A NEW CHAPTER

Sibanye Is Officially Registered

Sibanye (We Are One) has officially been registered as a political party. This is not the end of our journey. It is the beginning of our responsibility to listen to communities, represent their priorities and build a political organisation that remains connected to the people.

WHO WE ARE

We Are One

Sibanye is built around a simple principle: people must come first.

Sibanye (We Are One) is an officially registered political party rooted in the belief that government should listen to the people it serves.

We believe residents should have a meaningful voice in the decisions that affect their daily lives — from basic services and infrastructure to economic opportunities, community safety and accountable governance.

Our role is not simply to speak. Our role is to listen, understand, represent and act.

We want to build a political culture where residents are not only approached during elections, but are continuously heard and included in the conversations that shape their communities.

“

We listen to the people so their priorities can be represented.

Sibanye is a platform through which residents can raise concerns, share ideas and communicate the issues that matter most to them.

”
OUR PURPOSE

We Are the Voice That Listens

Sibanye exists to strengthen the connection between residents and the institutions responsible for serving them.

01

Listen

We listen to residents, communities, workers, young people, entrepreneurs and families to understand the challenges they experience.

02

Understand

We seek to understand the real causes of local problems rather than simply reacting to symptoms or making promises.

03

Represent

We take the priorities and concerns of residents into the political and democratic processes where decisions are made.

04

Hold Accountable

We believe residents deserve transparency, measurable commitments and accountability from those entrusted with public responsibility.

WHY SIBANYE

OUR PRIORITIES

What Matters to Our Communities

Our priorities are grounded in the issues residents experience in their everyday lives.

01

Water & Basic Services

Reliable water, sanitation, refuse collection and essential municipal services.

02

Roads & Infrastructure

Better maintenance of roads, public infrastructure and essential community facilities.

03

Jobs & Local Enterprise

Supporting local businesses, entrepreneurship, skills development and opportunities for young people.

04

Safe Communities

Supporting safer, cleaner and more organised communities through responsible local governance and community participation.

05

Transparency

Promoting openness, responsible public spending and meaningful accountability.

06

Community Participation

Creating stronger opportunities for residents to raise concerns, contribute ideas and participate in local democratic processes.
